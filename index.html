The original supplied diagram and text remain the foundation. Short descriptions are the existing game's teaching summaries, not invented quotations.

One load. Seven connected relationships.

4000 kg BEM + 200 kg crew + 200 kg operating items = 4400 kg DOM. Eight passengers (640), eight bags (160) and 200 kg cargo give 1000 kg Traffic Load. With 600 kg takeoff fuel, OM = 5000; ZFM = 5400; Useful Load = 1600; TOM = 6000. Add 50 kg taxi fuel for Taxi Mass = 6050; burn 400 kg trip fuel for LM = 5600 kg.

CG = total moment ÷ total mass. Moment = mass × arm. In this fictional geometry, datum is the nose; MAC starts at 4 m and is 3 m long. %MAC = 100 × (CG − 4) ÷ 3. These geometry extensions are not definitions quoted from Oxford.

Source limitations & terminology

The supplied text refers full BEM, DOM, OM, Traffic Load and MZFM definitions to CAP 696; the complete referenced material is not supplied. Maximum Structural Taxi Mass is also called Ramp Mass or Block Mass in the extract. MSTOM, PLTOM, MSLM, PLLM, Dry Operating Index and Fuel Index are named but not fully defined here. We preserve the source without inventing missing definitions or index conversions.

All new station geometry, limits and performance numbers belong to fictional RF–01, not a certified aircraft. Forward/aft CG outside this model's bounds restricts dispatch. Handling and stall recovery are not simulated.
