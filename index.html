    How the masses connect

    Plain-English help based on your uploaded diagram.

    BEM + crew and operating items = DOM
    DOM + takeoff fuel = OM
    DOM + Traffic Load = ZFM
    Traffic Load + takeoff fuel = Useful Load
    DOM + Useful Load = TOM
    TOM + start and taxi fuel = Taxi Mass
    TOM − trip fuel = LM

    A calculated mass is not a limit. ZFM is a value. MZFM is its maximum permitted value.

    The uploaded text calls Traffic Load the revenue-generating load, originally called “payload”. It defines Useful Load as Traffic Load plus takeoff fuel.

    Some full definitions are referred to CAP 696 in your text, but are not included in the upload. The short descriptions here are teaching summaries, not quotations from CAP 696. ZFM, TOM, and LM names are expanded from your requested terms.
